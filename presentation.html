Product 01

☁️ Cowork (Cloud)

Claude works like a real teammate in the cloud

What is Cowork?

Cowork is like giving Claude its own desk at your office. You give Claude a job, and it works on it in the background — like a real coworker! You can go do other things while Claude finishes the task.

Simple Example:

Imagine you tell your friend: "Hey, read these 50 reports and make a summary for me." Your friend goes away, reads everything, and comes back with the summary. That's Cowork!

  • 📋 Give Claude big tasks and it works alone (like a real employee)
  • ⏰ Claude works in the background — you don't have to wait
  • 👥 Share results with your whole team
  • 🔒 Everything stays safe and private in the cloud
  • 🔄 Claude can do research, write documents, analyze data
☁���👨‍💼
Your Cloud Teammate
Give tasks → Claude works → You get results

Available on: claude.ai (Pro, Team & Enterprise plans)
Best for: Big research tasks, data analysis, document creation

How Does Cowork... Work? 🤔

Step 1: You Give a Task 📝

You tell Claude what you need. Example: "Analyze our last 3 months of sales data and find trends."

Step 2: Claude Goes to Work 🏃

Claude starts working on it by itself. It reads data, thinks, and creates the output. You can close the tab!

Step 3: You Get Notified 🔔

When Claude finishes, you get a notification. Come back and see the results!

Step 4: Review & Share 📤

Check the work, make changes if needed, and share with your team.

Product 02

💻 Claude Code

Your AI coding partner that lives in the terminal

What is Claude Code?

Claude Code is a tool for programmers. It's like having a coding expert sitting next to you who can write code, fix mistakes (bugs), and build entire projects!

It works in the terminal — that black screen where programmers type commands. You describe what you want, and Claude writes the code.

Simple Example:

Imagine you say: "Build me a to-do list app with a blue theme." Claude Code will write ALL the code needed to make that app work. Like magic! ✨

  • ⌨️ Works right in your terminal (command line)
  • 📂 Understands your entire project and all your files
  • 🐛 Finds and fixes bugs (mistakes in code)
  • 🏗️ Can build entire features from scratch
  • 🔧 Works with Git (code saving system)
  • 🧪 Writes and runs tests automatically

How to Install Claude Code 📥

Step 1: Install Node.js 📦

Go to nodejs.org and download it. Node.js is like a helper tool that Claude Code needs to run.

Step 2: Open Terminal 🖥️

On Windows: Search "Command Prompt" or "PowerShell". On Mac: Open "Terminal" app.

Step 3: Type the Install Command ⌨️

Type: npm install -g @anthropic-ai/claude-code and press Enter.

Step 4: Start Using It! 🎉

Type claude in any project folder to start coding with Claude!

Product 03

🔗 Claude Connectors

Connect Claude to your company's knowledge and tools

What are Claude Connectors?

Imagine Claude is a new employee at your company. Right now, Claude doesn't know anything about YOUR company. Connectors are like giving Claude access to the company's filing cabinet!

With Connectors, Claude can read your Google Drive files, Notion pages, Slack messages, and more. So when you ask Claude a question about your company, it actually knows the answer!

Simple Example:

Without Connectors: You ask "What's our return policy?" Claude says: "I don't know your company's policy." 😕

With Connectors: You ask the same thing. Claude reads your company docs and says: "Your return policy is 30 days with receipt!" ✅

  • 📁 Connects to Google Drive, Notion, Confluence, Sharepoint
  • 💬 Reads Slack messages and channels
  • 🔍 Claude can search through all your company's documents
  • 🔒 Keeps data secure — only authorized people can access
  • 🏢 Available for Enterprise (big company) plans

Product 04

🧠 Claude Skills

Teach Claude custom tricks and actions

What are Claude Skills?

Think of Skills like teaching Claude a recipe. You tell Claude: "Whenever I say 'write a blog post', follow these exact steps and use this style."

Skills are saved instructions that tell Claude exactly how to do a specific job. Instead of explaining every time, you create a Skill once, and use it forever!

Simple Example:

🍕 Without Skill: Every time you order pizza, you explain: "I want thin crust, extra cheese, no onions..."

🍕 With Skill: You just say "My usual!" and they know exactly what to make. That's a Skill!

  • 📋 Create saved instructions for specific tasks
  • 🔄 Use them again and again without re-explaining
  • 👥 Share skills with your team
  • 📊 Skills can create charts, tables, and formatted outputs
  • 🔧 Customize Claude's behavior for specific workflows
📄 skills.md — Example Skill File
# Blog Post Writer Skill

## What this skill does:
Name: Blog Post Writer
Trigger: "Write a blog about {topic}"

## Instructions:
1. Write a catchy title
2. Add an introduction (3 lines)
3. Write 3 main sections
4. End with a conclusion
5. Keep tone friendly & simple

Style: Casual, fun, easy to read
Length: 500-800 words

Claude as a real app on your computer

Simple Example:

Product 06

🌐 Claude in Chrome

Claude right inside your Chrome browser

What is Claude in Chrome?

It's a Chrome extension — a small add-on for your Chrome browser. Once installed, Claude appears as a little button on any webpage you visit!

Reading a long article? Click Claude and say "summarize this." Writing an email? Claude helps you write better. It works on ANY website!

Simple Example:

🔍 You're reading a Wikipedia page about space. It's 20 pages long! Click the Claude button → "Summarize this in 5 sentences" → Done! You get a short summary in seconds. 🚀

  • 📄 Summarize any webpage in seconds
  • ✍️ Help you write emails, comments, and posts
  • 🌏 Translate content on any page
  • ❓ Ask questions about what's on the page
  • 📋 Extract key information from articles
  • 🎨 Doesn't slow down your browser
🌐✨
Browse + Chat = Magic!

How to install:

1️⃣ Open Chrome browser
2️⃣ Go to Chrome Web Store
3️⃣ Search "Claude by Anthropic"
4️⃣ Click "Add to Chrome"
5️⃣ Done! Claude icon appears in toolbar 🎉

My Thoughts

🎯 Personal Takeaways

What I learned from this research

01

Claude is Everywhere Now

Claude is not just a chatbot anymore. It's in your browser, your desktop, your terminal, your company's tools. Anthropic is making Claude available wherever you work. This is really smart because the best AI is the one that's always within reach.

02

Claude Code is a Game-Changer

As someone learning about programming, Claude Code amazed me. It can understand an entire project and write code for you. This doesn't replace programmers — it makes them 10x faster. Junior developers can learn faster with it!

03

Skills Make Claude Personal

Skills are my favorite feature. Instead of generic AI, you get YOUR AI that knows YOUR way of doing things. It's like training a new employee — once trained, they work perfectly every time.

04

Connectors = Real Business Value

Most AI chatbots don't know about YOUR company. Connectors solve this. When Claude can read your company's files, it becomes truly useful for real work — not just general questions. This is huge for businesses.

05

Cowork = The Future of Work

The idea that Claude works in the background like a real teammate is powerful. Give it a task, go have lunch, come back to finished work. This will change how teams operate. AI is becoming a real coworker, not just a tool.

06

Easy to Start, Hard to Master

All these products are easy to start using, but getting the most out of them takes practice. The key is to experiment! Try different prompts, create skills, and use Claude in your daily workflow. The more you use it, the better it gets.
